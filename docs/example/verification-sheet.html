Verification sheet — Gareth Huggins feature

How the feature was prepared

From the founder's interview (paraphrased voice notes on 2 and 3 October 2026 and his typed messages on 4 October), his own knowledge base and project records (read-only), the public record (Companies House), the live websites of his businesses, his clients and The Link Network, and read-only records from his organisation's code hosting (repository dates, pull-request counts, product READMEs). No client, family member, agency, startup founder or other third party was contacted. One AI evidence check and one AI editorial read were run; neither is external verification.

Status key

Public record: an independent official source. Public page: a company's own live page (its own voice). Record: a dated document, repository or delivery record in the founder's own systems. Account: the founder's account, not independently corroborated. Design: documented process or intent, not shown in live operation. Writer: the writer's interpretation.
