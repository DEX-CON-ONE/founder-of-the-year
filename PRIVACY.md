# Privacy

**What this plugin is.** Plain Markdown instructions: a skill, four agents and reference files. It contains no executable code, no hooks, no MCP servers and no telemetry.

**What happens to your data.** Everything you tell your agent, and every file it reads while using the skill, goes to your model provider under that provider's terms. The plugin does not send anything anywhere itself. Agents may use your agent's web search and fetch tools if you authorise delegation; they read public pages and do not sign in, submit forms or contact anyone.

**What it writes.** An assignment folder in your project: a `public/` folder of drafts and a `private/` folder of interview records, claim ledger and research. The private folder can hold personal and confidential material. Keep it out of version control and out of anything you share.

**Third parties.** An interview can involve people other than you (family, clients, collaborators). The skill records restrictions and permissions, and keeps unnamed anything not cleared, but responsibility for what you publish is yours. Obtain consent before naming anyone.

**Contact.** Open an issue in this repository.
