# Assignment folder and continuity

Create these in the assignment folder, never inside the skill. Fill in what sources already establish; ask only for what is materially missing. No placeholder text in anything under `public/`.

## Layout

```
founder-feature/<subject>/
  public/
    feature.md                 the article (canonical text)
    feature.html               the designed page built from it
    feature.pdf                printed from the HTML
    verification-sheet.md      sanitised claim map for an editor
    bios.md                    short and fuller biographies
    fact-sheet.md              each business: what, for whom, stage, link
    editorial-brief.md         (optional) angle, headline rationale, tone
    story-brief.md             (optional) one page for an editor
    pitch-draft.md             (optional) unsent
    quotes-and-visuals.md      (optional) exact quotes, assets, rights, photo brief
    promo-pack.md              (optional, after review) channel-ready copy
  private/
    assignment.md              the brief (below)
    gathered.md                what the founder's own systems already told you
    session.md                 state and handoff
    interview/session-NN.md    dated interview records
    claim-ledger.tsv           every claim, its source and status
    open-questions.md          the one batch of questions, with defaults
    narrative-options.md       candidate stories and the choice
    research/                  researcher registers, one file per question
    review-log.md              what was checked, what changed
    legacy/                    superseded drafts imported from earlier work
    promo-claims-check.tsv     (if a promo pack exists) each line mapped to a ledger ID
```

## Importing earlier work

If the founder has a handbook, an earlier draft, an interview record or research from another tool, bring it in rather than starting again. Copy useful files into `private/` (originals untouched, locations recorded), keep interview records as `private/interview/session-NN.md`, extract every restriction into `session.md` in the founder's own words, and move superseded drafts to `private/legacy/`. Do not copy anything carrying credentials or private links; reference it by location.

## assignment.md

- Subject and preferred public name; the businesses and how they relate (entities, brands, clients, board or partner roles).
- Reader, register and any target outlet (conventions only; never an implied affiliation).
- Outputs wanted and the stage requested.
- Boundaries: who may be named, what stays private, approved personal themes, in the founder's words.
- Sources available and their limits; delegation authorised or not.

## session.md

Rewrite after every answer or pause so a fresh session resumes without re-asking: date; position; latest decisions and restrictions (verbatim where known); confirmed, attributed and conflicting facts; the next question; reviews run and what remains.

## Handing off to another tool or chat

Give the brief, the relevant files or their locations, the latest decisions, the interview record and the next step explicitly. Sanitise for the receiving context. Say which sources and agents are not available there.
