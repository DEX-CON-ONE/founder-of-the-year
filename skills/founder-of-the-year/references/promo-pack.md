# Promotional pack

Channel-ready copy derived from the reviewed article and the ledger. A promotional line may be shorter and punchier than the article; it may never be stronger. If a sentence cannot be traced to a ledger entry that supports it, it does not go in.

Produce it only after the article has been through its check, and only for the audiences the founder named. If none were named, ask once (buyers, partners, investors, peers, press) and tailor to those.

## Contents (`public/promo-pack.md`; scale to the channels in play)

1. **Message house.** One honest positioning line; three proof points, each on a corroborated or clearly attributed ledger entry; the things this founder must not claim.
2. **Profile copy.** Headline options (keep the founder's exact current headline unless they ask to change it), an About rewrite, a Featured plan. Roles and entities match the fact sheet word for word.
3. **Posts.** A launch post for the article and three to five evergreen posts, each on one concrete decision or example, in the founder's own voice, with the ledger or verification-sheet IDs they rest on.
4. **Website copy.** Boilerplate, short bio, the article's headline and standfirst, an honest label.
5. **Short video brief.** A beat sheet and caption script for a 15 to 25 second teaser: hook, scenes, on-screen text, the ID behind every claim shown. Captions assumed.
6. **Small assets.** Email signature line, speaker introduction, one-line introduction.

## Rules

- Keep a private `promo-claims-check.tsv` mapping each factual line to its ledger ID and treatment.
- Maturity words identical everywhere: planned, built, running, launched, measured.
- No borrowed authority: no award, ranking, endorsement, publication or affiliation; no third-party logos; no "verified" unless an independent party did it.
- Each asset carries a short visible "Founder-prepared with AI assistance" line.
- Third parties named only where the ledger records permission; check combinations of details that could identify someone.
- Guidance addressed to the founder (audience notes, tone, warnings about their own sites) goes in `private/promo-guidance.md`, not the public pack.
- Nothing is posted or scheduled by the skill. Re-read each channel's rules on the day.

Run the pack reviewer on the promotional pack once, asking specifically whether any asset says more than the article does.
