# Interviewing the founder

The interview is for what records cannot tell you: motivation, the hard decision, what changed for someone, what did not work. Read `private/gathered.md` and the earlier records first and never ask for a fact a system already holds.

## Three modes

- **Prepare.** Write a short guide to `private/interview/guide.md` (main questions, follow-ups, the purpose of each) and a one-line launch prompt. Stop before asking question one.
- **Interview.** Ask one question, as ordinary chat text, and wait. The founder may dictate, so expect long, loose, mis-transcribed answers; check an odd-looking name or number rather than guessing. Keep your own turns short. Do not draft the article mid-interview.
- **Resume.** Read `private/session.md`, pick up from the last meaningful answer. Do not restart with the origin story.

Use the structured question tool only for a discrete choice (which date, may this be named). Stories need free text.

## Question routes

Adapt; skip any route the sources already answer.

| Topic | Main question | Follow the answer |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | What made you commit to this? | Gradual: what accumulated and what was the first action? A moment: what were the options and what did you do next? |
| Need | Who has the problem you are solving? | One person's obstacle; what it stopped them doing |
| Experience | Which earlier experience changed how you approached it? | One decision where that lesson mattered, not a list of jobs |
| Choice | A decision where the options had different costs or risks | Why reject the other; who was involved; what happened after |
| Value | One piece of work that shows what changed for someone | Before, after, who did what, what record shows the difference |
| Reality | What can someone use today? | Separate planned, built, deployed, in use; what is unresolved |
| Learning | What has not worked as you hoped? | What changed in your approach |
| Direction | What would progress look like next? | Which is a commitment, which an aspiration |

Probes when needed: "What exactly happened?", "Which part was your contribution?", "How do you know?", "What would someone who disagrees say?" For a metric, ask for baseline, period, units and the record behind it; "not yet measurable" is an acceptable answer.

Where several businesses are involved, ask why they are separate and how they reinforce each other. Confirm entities, brands, clients and advisory roles separately; a client's project is not the founder's venture.

## Boundaries and sensitivity

If the founder says something is private, record it in `private/session.md` in their words and exclude it from every public output, including any sentence explaining what was left out. If an answer touches bereavement, health, family or money, acknowledge it, let them steer, and ask their publication preference before any of it is written. Do not probe circumstances. Never introduce a guessed personal or financial narrative.

## Ending a session

Check important facts and publication boundaries briefly. Save `private/interview/session-NN.md`, labelling the record verbatim, paraphrased or reconstructed, with exact quote candidates and their locations. Refresh `private/session.md` with position, confirmed facts, new restrictions and the next question. Never reconstruct missing speech.
