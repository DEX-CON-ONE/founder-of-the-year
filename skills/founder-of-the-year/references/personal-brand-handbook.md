# Personal brand handbook (optional stage)

A handbook fixes positioning, entity relationships, public and private boundaries and the claims that still need evidence. It expresses agreed positioning; it never corroborates an achievement. Most article assignments do not need one; do it when the founder asks for it or has no settled positioning at all.

## Reuse before creating

If a handbook, copy bank or earlier positioning exists, record its location, date and gaps in `private/assignment.md` and use it as the foundation. Recheck a fact only when it is stale, conflicting or needed for publication. Do not rerun discovery to satisfy a workflow order.

## Create when asked

From the gathered material, public records and a short interview, write `public/handbook.md` with:

- Positioning and purpose: who the founder helps, the problem, the useful difference, the evidence behind it.
- Brands and roles: legal entities, public businesses, clients, partnerships, advisory roles.
- The founder story: experiences that shaped judgement and decisions, not a CV and not an invented origin scene.
- Audience messages, headline and About drafts, reusable biographies, consistent with the founder's chosen emphasis.
- Public and private boundaries.

Evidence, conflicts and private plans go in `private/handbook-evidence.md` and `private/strategy.md`, not the handbook. If asked to build from the founder's actual current headline, get its exact text; never replace it silently.

## Then

Record the handbook's status and gaps, and move to gathering and the one batch of questions. Update the handbook later only within the scope the founder asked for.
