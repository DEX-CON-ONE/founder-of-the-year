# The pack around the article

Scale it to the assignment. The article plus the verification sheet is the minimum; add the rest only when it is useful to the founder.

## Always

1. **Feature** (`feature.md`, `feature.html`, `feature.pdf`): the article, as described in `finished-founder-feature.md` and `article-design.md`.
2. **Verification sheet** (`verification-sheet.md`): the claim map an editor would want, sanitised. For each consequential statement in the article: what it says, the basis (public record, company page, founder's account, design, writer's interpretation), and what is still needed. Plus: what was deliberately not used and why (without repeating the figure), the naming permissions still open, and the further reporting an editor would need. No private paths, links, figures or confidential terms. Never export the full ledger.

## Usually

3. **Bios** (`bios.md`): a short one (about 45 words) and a fuller one (about 150), consistent with the article's roles and stages.
4. **Fact sheet** (`fact-sheet.md`): each business in a row: what it does, for whom, current stage, public link. Future plans separate.

## When asked, or when there is a real reason

5. **Story brief**: one page on why it matters now, who it affects, current stage, evidence available and not available, and what an editor could do next. An interview request is not itself news.
6. **Pitch draft**: unsent, concise, tailored to a real beat, with no invented recipient or contact and no promise of access the founder has not authorised.
7. **Quotes and visuals**: the exact quotes used with their sources; available assets, credits and rights; a photo brief if no usable image exists.
8. **Promotional pack**: see `promo-pack.md`; only after the article has been reviewed.

Do not add a press release unless there is a genuine announcement. An award-entry narrative needs the organiser's criteria and is not evidence of winning.

## Labels and consistency

Every public document carries "Founder-prepared, drafted with AI assistance" once. Roles, stages and maturity words (planned, built, running, launched, measured) must be identical across the article and every pack document. Superseded drafts move to `private/legacy/`, never stay in `public/`.

## Handover

A few plain lines: what exists and where, how far it got (ready for the founder's read, or waiting on named decisions), and the one or two decisions still theirs. Nothing is sent, submitted or published by the skill.
